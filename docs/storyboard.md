# Storyboard

**Working title:** *Fewer thefts, more reported violence: what ten years of police records say about crime in France (2016–2025)*

**Audience:** local decision-makers (prefectures, départements, city councils) and journalists/citizens.
**Goal:** replace "is crime going up?" with a sharper question: *which* crime, *where*, and how much of the rise is better reporting?

| # | Chapter (story beat) | Headline the viewer should remember | Visual(s) | Interaction | Question answered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook**: Why it matters | "Crime is not one number. Its shape changed over the decade." | Text, audience/questions cards, warning callout | none | Why should I care? |
| 2 | **Context**: The big picture | "Recorded violence against persons +84%, property crime −14% (2016→2025)." | 5 KPI tiles; line chart of 5 families indexed to 2016 = 100 with #MeToo, Grenelle and COVID annotations; diverging bar chart of change per indicator | Period, regions, overseas toggle, focus indicator (sidebar) | Q1 What changed? |
| 3a | **Insight**: Where | "Sexual violence rose in all 101 départements." | Choropleth map (rate or % change), tables of largest/smallest increases | Map toggle *Rate / Change*; hover | Q2 Where? |
| 3b | **Comparison**: regions | "Same direction everywhere, different levels." | Small multiples, one panel per region, with the selection average dotted | Sidebar filters | Q2 Where? |
| 3c | **Ranking & distribution** | "Top vs bottom decile differ 1.4–2.8×; overseas départements and Paris stand out." | Top/bottom N bars; box plot per year with every département; scatter of rate vs population with fit and outliers | Top-N slider; hover | Q3 Who stands out? |
| 3d | **Drill-down** | "Each département has its own profile." | Ratio-to-average bars for all indicators; trend vs average | Département selector | Q3 Who? |
| 4 | **Explanation**: What if reporting changed? | "If reporting doubled, about +27% of the sexual-violence rise remains real." | Decomposition bars (recorded / reporting / implied actual); estimate of unrecorded victims | Reporting-change slider; reporting-rate slider | Q4 Why? |
| 5 | **Trust**: Data quality | "9/10 checks pass; the warning concerns interpretation." | Checks table, missingness, sparsity heatmap, limitations | Downloads | Can I trust it? |
| 6 | **Implications & next steps** | "Plan for more victims coming forward; don't report crime as one number." | Insight / implication cards, ethics | none | So what? |

**Flow:** Hook → Context → Insight → Explanation → Trust → Implications. Every chapter opens with a one-sentence headline computed from the data, so it stays true when the filters change.
